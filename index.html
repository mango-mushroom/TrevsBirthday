01 / Getting there

Don't drive. Edith is a one-block alley off Union, and street parking on Fleet Week weekend is a rumour. Rideshare, or take the 30 / 45 up Columbus and walk the last block. {{ doorLine }}

02 / The Blue Angels

Friday is Fleet Week's first show day and the deck faces the whole run. Expect the jets over the bay mid-afternoon — roughly three to four o'clock, weather permitting. It is very loud and completely worth it; bring earplugs if you're sound-sensitive.
