01 / Getting there

Don't drive. Edith is a one-block alley off Union, and street parking on Fleet Week weekend is a rumour. Rideshare, or take the 30 / 45 up Columbus and walk the last block. {{ doorLine }}

02 / The Blue Angels

Friday is Fleet Week's first show day and the deck faces the whole run. Expect the jets over the bay mid-afternoon — roughly three to four o'clock, weather permitting. It is very loud and completely worth it; bring earplugs if you're sound-sensitive.

03 / What to bring

A bottle to share, or a dish if you're inclined.

Whatever you actually like to drink — ice and glassware are inside.

A warm layer. The wind arrives with the sunset, every time.

No gifts. Trev has enough things; he'd rather have the company.

04 / Roof rules
01No glass on the deck — cans, cups and plastic only.
02Stay off the railing. Don't sit on it, don't lean on it.
03Music comes down at 10 PM. House rule, not a suggestion.
04Quiet in the stairwell — there are neighbours the whole way down.
05Never prop the roof door. It locks for a reason.
06Carry your empties back down with you on the way out.
